Status logic: Basic info is required before the provider can appear in results. Self-declared coaching and photo/video become live at submission. Certified coaching requires approval for the badge; while pending, customers can find the provider under “Show all” as experienced / uncertified. Dashboard actions are scoped to individual modules.
