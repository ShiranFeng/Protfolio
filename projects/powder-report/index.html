Ski Butler — A Concierge for People Who Just Want to Have Fun

This project imagines a concierge platform that pairs riders with locals who'll show them around, take their photos, and drive them there — no lesson required, no commitment, just someone who's done this a hundred times taking the hassle off your hands.

01 / Discover

The hassles around the riding

Three years of snowboarding taught me one thing above all: skiing is full of small, dumb hassles that have nothing to do with skiing itself — scattered weather info, no one around to film you, losing your friends on the mountain.

02 / Define

One framework, three kinds of help

Scoped around three booking types — a "ski butler" companion role (guiding, photos/video, light pointers — not formal instruction), carpooling, and formal instruction — sharing one framework: provider profile, filter by resort + date, message to confirm, pay, a day-of "find each other" moment, mutual review after.

A companion, not a lesson

The ski butler role is being designed for a different customer entirely — someone who doesn't want the commitment of a lesson, just wants a smooth, guided day and good photos to show for it. Selection criteria leans on experience and a sample portfolio rather than certification.

03 / Develop

Starting with instructor booking

Built the instructor booking flow first: providers list which resorts they travel to, what they teach (snowboard/ski), whether they're certified (certified providers go through review before going live; uncertified "experienced" providers go live immediately, filtered by reviews over time)

— customers filter by riding style, skill level, and can choose to see certified-only or everyone.

04 / Next steps · In progress

Connecting the rest of the day

Carpooling is scoped as the next module — matched by route and time rather than by picking a person, with gear space as a ski-specific detail generic carpool apps don't need.
